Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

1. Zero-Cloud & Local Storage Philosophy

VantaLock is engineered on a strict zero-cloud, local-first architecture. We do not operate remote databases, vault syncing servers, or cloud storage backends. All files, passwords, notes, and cryptographic master keys encrypted by VantaLock remain strictly stored on your local disk.

2. Data Collection & Telemetry

We do not track you. VantaLock collects:

  • Zero personal information: No names, email addresses, usernames, or contact details are collected by the application.
  • Zero telemetry or usage analytics: The desktop software does not contain tracking scripts, crash telemetry reporting, or behavioral analytics.
  • Zero keystroke or vault data: Cryptographic operations occur in local memory. Master passwords and seed phrases are never transmitted over any network.

3. Open Source Transparency

The VantaLock source code is publicly accessible on GitHub. Anyone can review the code to verify our zero-tracking policy and cryptographic implementation.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our security design, please contact us at support@vantalocksecure.com.
